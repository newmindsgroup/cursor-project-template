# Brand Identity Audit

Evaluate brand identity consistency and completeness using a systematic approach. A cohesive brand identity builds trust, recognition, and emotional connection—essential for website effectiveness.

## Purpose

Audit and document brand identity to:
- Ensure visual and verbal consistency across the website
- Identify gaps in brand assets before implementation
- Guide design decisions with clear brand standards
- Create a foundation for all marketing touchpoints

## When to Use

- During project discovery to assess existing brand
- Before starting visual design work
- When inconsistencies appear across materials
- When refreshing or evolving a brand
- As input to design system development

## The Brand Identity Process

A complete brand identity follows five phases:

```
1. RESEARCH        2. STRATEGY        3. DESIGN          4. TOUCHPOINTS     5. MANAGEMENT
   ↓                  ↓                  ↓                  ↓                  ↓
Vision/Mission    Positioning        Logo System        Website            Guidelines
Audit             Attributes         Color              Marketing          Assets
Competition       Architecture       Typography         Environmental      Governance
Stakeholders      Naming             Imagery            Product            Evolution
```
