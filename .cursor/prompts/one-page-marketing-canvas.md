# One-Page Marketing Canvas

A complete marketing strategy on one page, covering the entire customer journey from stranger to raving fan. This canvas ensures website decisions are grounded in comprehensive marketing strategy.

## Purpose

Create a unified marketing strategy document that:
- Captures the complete customer journey (before, during, after)
- Informs website content, features, and architecture
- Aligns all marketing activities toward conversion
- Provides context for all team members and stakeholders

## When to Use

- At project kickoff to establish marketing foundations
- When developing content strategy
- Before designing landing pages or funnels
- When reviewing marketing effectiveness
- To align stakeholders on marketing approach

```markdown
| [Magnet 2] | PDF/Video/Tool | _______________ | _______________ |

### Lead Magnet Requirements
For each lead magnet, it must:
- [ ] Solve a specific, immediate problem
- [ ] Be consumable in <10 minutes
- [ ] Demonstrate expertise
- [ ] Naturally lead to paid offering
- [ ] Have high perceived value

### Opt-in Locations
Where will lead capture forms appear?
- [ ] Homepage
- [ ] Blog posts
- [ ] Dedicated landing pages
- [ ] Exit intent pop-ups
- [ ] Sidebar/footer
- [ ] Content upgrades

### Form Fields Strategy
| Form Purpose | Fields | Conversion Goal |
|--------------|--------|-----------------|
| Newsletter | Email only | High volume |
| Lead magnet | Email, First name | Medium volume |
| Consultation | Email, Name, Phone, Need | Qualified leads |

### Lead Scoring (Optional)
How will you qualify leads?
- [ ] Engagement scoring
- [ ] Form field qualification
- [ ] Behavior tracking
- [ ] Lead source tracking
```
