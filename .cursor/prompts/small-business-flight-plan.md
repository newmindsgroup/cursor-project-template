# Small Business Flight Plan Assessment

A 6-pillar diagnostic that evaluates every aspect of a business before and during website projects. The website is one component of a complete business operating system—this assessment ensures all pillars are considered.

## Purpose

Evaluate a client's business across all six pillars to:
- Understand where the website fits in the overall business system
- Identify which pillars need attention before/alongside website work
- Scope website features based on business maturity
- Provide holistic consulting beyond just website development

## When to Use

- During initial project discovery
- When scoping a comprehensive digital transformation
- Before recommending marketing automation
- When positioning website work within larger business consulting

## The Flight Plan Metaphor

Think of a business as an airplane:

```
                    ┌──────────────────┐
                    │    LEADERSHIP    │  ← Cockpit (Direction)
                    │   (The Cockpit)  │
                    └────────┬─────────┘
                             │
         ┌───────────────────┼───────────────────┐
         │                   │                   │
    ┌────┴────┐         ┌────┴────┐         ┌────┴────┐
    │MARKETING│         │  BODY   │         │  SALES  │
    │(R Engine)│         │PRODUCTS│         │(L Engine)│
    └─────────┘         │   OPS   │         └─────────┘
                        └────┬────┘
                             │
                    ┌────────┴────────┐
                    │    CASH FLOW    │  ← Fuel Tanks
                    │  (Fuel Tanks)   │
                    └─────────────────┘
```

- **Cockpit (Leadership)**: Sets direction, without it you're lost
- **Right Engine (Marketing)**: Brings fuel (leads) into the plane
- **Left Engine (Sales)**: Converts fuel into forward thrust
- **Wings (Products)**: Provides lift to stay airborne
- **Body (Operations)**: The structure that holds everything together
- **Fuel Tanks (Cash Flow)**: The resource that keeps you flying

## Pillar 1: Leadership

**Function:** Cast vision, align team, set direction

### Assessment Questions

```
Rate each 1-5 (1 = not present, 5 = fully developed)

MISSION AND VISION
- [ ] Clear mission statement exists: ___/5
- [ ] Vision for 3-5 years articulated: ___/5
- [ ] Team knows and believes in mission: ___/5
- [ ] Mission drives daily decisions: ___/5

GUIDING PRINCIPLES
- [ ] Core values documented: ___/5
- [ ] Values influence hiring decisions: ___/5
- [ ] Values reflected in customer experience: ___/5

LEADERSHIP RHYTHM
- [ ] Regular team meetings occur: ___/5
- [ ] Clear communication channels exist: ___/5
- [ ] Goals are tracked and reviewed: ___/5

Leadership Score: ___/50
```

### Website Implications

| Leadership Gap | Website Consideration |
|---------------|----------------------|
| Unclear mission | About page needs development work first |
| No guiding principles | Brand voice will be inconsistent |
| Poor communication | Client portal/updates may not be maintained |
