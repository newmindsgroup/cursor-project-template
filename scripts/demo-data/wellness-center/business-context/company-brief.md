# Serenity Wellness Center - Company Brief

## About Us

Serenity Wellness Center is a premier day spa and wellness facility located in Scottsdale, Arizona. Founded in 2018 by Sarah Chen, a licensed massage therapist with 15 years of experience, we've grown from a small massage studio to a full-service wellness destination.

## Our Mission

To provide a sanctuary where clients can escape the stresses of daily life and restore their physical, mental, and emotional balance through expert treatments and personalized care.

## Current Challenges

1. **Outdated Website** - Our current website looks dated and doesn't reflect the premium experience we provide
2. **Low Online Bookings** - Only 15% of appointments come through online booking
3. **Unclear Service Information** - Clients often call to understand service differences
4. **No Mobile Optimization** - Site is difficult to use on phones

## Goals for New Website

1. Increase online bookings to 50%+ of total appointments
2. Clearly communicate our service offerings and expertise
3. Establish Serenity as the premier wellness destination in Scottsdale
4. Create a calming, luxurious digital experience that matches our physical space
5. Easy-to-find pricing and booking information

## Target Audience

- Value quality over price

### Secondary: Self-Care Enthusiasts (25-45)
