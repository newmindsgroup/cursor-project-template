# StoryBrand Framework Guide

The StoryBrand framework, created by Donald Miller, helps clarify your message so customers will listen. Every piece of content follows this 7-part framework where **your customer is the hero** and **your brand is the guide**.

---

## The 7 Elements of StoryBrand

### 1. A Character (The Hero = Your Customer)

**Who is your customer and what do they want?**

- Define your ideal customer clearly
- Identify their primary desire related to your offering
- Keep it simple: ONE clear desire

**Example:**
> "Small business owners who want to grow their online presence without technical headaches."

---

### 2. Has a Problem

Every hero faces an obstacle. Your customer has problems on three levels:

| Level | Description | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| **External** | The tangible, surface-level problem | "I don't have a website" |
| **Internal** | How the problem makes them feel | "I feel overwhelmed and behind" |
| **Philosophical** | Why it's just plain wrong | "Every business deserves a professional online presence" |

**The Villain:** What's the root cause? (Complexity, confusion, lack of time, etc.)

---

### 3. And Meets a Guide (Your Brand)

Your brand enters as the guide, NOT the hero. Guides have two qualities:

**Empathy:** "We understand your frustration."
- Show you've been there
- Acknowledge their pain
- Connect emotionally

**Authority:** "We've helped hundreds of businesses like yours."
- Testimonials
- Statistics
- Credentials
- Experience

---

### 4. Who Gives Them a Plan

Reduce confusion with a simple process (3-4 steps max):

1. **Step 1:** Schedule a consultation
2. **Step 2:** Get your custom strategy
3. **Step 3:** Launch your new website
4. **Step 4:** Watch your business grow

Plans reduce risk and increase trust.

---

### 5. And Calls Them to Action

Two types of CTAs:

| Type | Purpose | Examples |
|------|---------|----------|
| **Direct CTA** | The buy button | "Get Started", "Schedule Now", "Buy Today" |
| **Transitional CTA** | Nurture leads | "Download Guide", "Watch Demo", "Learn More" |

**Rule:** Always have a clear, visible direct CTA.

---

### 6. That Helps Them Avoid Failure

What's at stake if they don't act?

- Lost revenue
- Wasted time
- Falling behind competitors
- Missed opportunities

**Don't overdo it** - just enough to create urgency.

---

### 7. And Ends in Success

Paint the picture of transformation:

**Before → After**

- Frustrated → Confident
- Overwhelmed → In control
- Invisible → Visible
- Struggling → Thriving

**Success Outcomes:**
- More leads
- Higher revenue
- Better work-life balance
- Professional reputation

---

## Applying StoryBrand to Website Sections

| Section | StoryBrand Element |
|---------|-------------------|
| **Hero** | Character's desire + Direct CTA |
| **Problem/Features** | Problem + Your solution (Guide) |
| **How It Works** | The Plan |
| **Testimonials** | Authority (Guide) |
| **Stats** | Authority (Guide) |
| **FAQ** | Overcome objections (reduce failure fear) |
| **CTA Section** | Call to Action + Success vision |

---

## Writing Tips

### Headlines
- Focus on the transformation, not features
- Use "you" language
- Keep under 10 words

**Bad:** "Our Award-Winning Web Design Services"
**Good:** "Get a Website That Grows Your Business"

### Body Copy
- Short sentences
- Active voice
- Benefit-focused
- No jargon

### CTAs
- Action verbs
- Specific outcomes
- Create urgency without being pushy

---

## Multilingual Considerations

When translating StoryBrand content:

1. **Adapt, don't just translate** - Cultural nuances matter
2. **Keep the framework** - The 7 elements work in any language
3. **Maintain character limits** - Some languages expand (German) or contract (Chinese)
4. **Use native speakers** - Especially for empathy statements
5. **Test CTAs** - Action words vary culturally
