About Our Mission

We're on a mission to bridge the gap between design and development with powerful prototyping tools and seamless handoff.

Our Story

We started with a simple observation: too much time was wasted in the handoff between design and development. Designers spent hours creating Figma annotations, developers struggled to interpret specs, and clients waited longer for results.

The Prototype Starter Kit was born from this frustration. By building directly in code with design tokens and automated export, we've eliminated the guesswork and created a workflow that's faster, more accurate, and more enjoyable for everyone.

Today, teams around the world use our kit to prototype faster, deliver pixel-perfect implementations, and spend more time on what matters: creating exceptional user experiences.
