Content Review Mode

Review the website structure and content without colors or images. This helps focus on messaging, hierarchy, and layout before visual design.

How to Use Wireframe Mode

1

Focus on Content

Review headlines, body copy, and calls-to-action without color or imagery distractions.

2

Check Hierarchy

Verify the visual hierarchy of information makes sense without relying on color cues.

3

Note Placeholders

Striped boxes indicate where images and videos will go. Review what content is needed.

Toggle wireframe mode on any page:
Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + W or ?wireframe=1
